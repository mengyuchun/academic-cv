A think-tank project on the professional development of children's-publishing editors.

<!--more-->

Contributed to questionnaire design (a 70-item editor survey with 257 valid responses; a 46-item management survey with 26 responses), conducted field and online interviews with 34 editors and executives, and transcribed verbatim records. Led the substantive rewriting of the final report, expanding a draft to a full-length study and restructuring its argument.
