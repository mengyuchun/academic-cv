A provincial-level research project using web-scraped reviewer comments from CSSCI journals to model peer-review concerns.

<!--more-->

Collected approximately 63,000 reviewer comments from the Wanwei Bookkeeping journal submission portal. Embedded comments with Sentence-BERT (`all-mpnet-base-v2`), tuned BERTopic with Bayesian optimization, and identified seven core topics such as reviewer bias and implicit rejection. Applied a Fengshenbang pre-trained model for sentiment quantification, finding 56.8% negative sentiment and revealing cyclical variation in submission behavior.
