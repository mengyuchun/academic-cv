abstract: >-
  Against the backdrop of artificial intelligence accelerating changes in
  content production, short dramas are at a critical stage of transformation
  from sensory stimulation toward high-quality narrative. This study collects
  the 20 most-viewed hit short dramas (Top20) listed on the official Hongguo
  Short Drama website and constructs a candidate dataset of 1,524 short dramas
  by scraping the site. It selects mid-tier short dramas (Sub20) with the same
  themes but weaker market performance to form a matched comparison group.
  Using multimodal computational communication methods, the study extracts and
  compares features across rhythm, narrative, and audiovisual dimensions. No
  significant between-group differences are detected in speech rate, loudness,
  or average shot length. The clearest differences lie in the face-to-frame
  area ratio and textual narration. Visually, top-performing works use a lower
  face-to-frame area ratio, leaving more space for environmental and other
  contextual information. Textually, they improve narrative effectiveness
  through a high density of power-related symbols and positive verbs. Based on
  these findings, the study proposes recommendations for short-drama creation
  in three areas: content, form, and production.
