An independent comparative study of eight leading Chinese and international generative-AI platforms.

<!--more-->

Selected eight platforms (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, and domestic Wenxin Yiyan, Doubao, iFlytek Spark, Kimi, and GLM-4) and built a 16-dimension content-analysis coding scheme. Two-coder inter-coder reliability reached Kappa = 0.896. The study identifies a divergence between retention and alienation models for output copyright, highlights domestic compliance gaps in the right to erasure and change-of-terms notice, and proposes an Opt-out mechanism as a policy recommendation.
